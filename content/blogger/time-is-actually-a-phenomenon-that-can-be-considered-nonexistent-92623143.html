 Time is Actually a Phenomenon That Can Be Considered Nonexistent




There are many legends about time; one of these is that the faster we travel, the more it slows down. But has any of us tried to draw a conclusion from the speed of light, which we consider to be the maximum speed, and from the equation E=mc², which most of us accept as true? No? Let me explain it this way: according to this equation, a massive particle traveling at the speed of light reaches such immense energy that it causes a warping in space-time. And what happens in this case? Specifically, there is no such concept as time. That is, that object has now reached infinity. It is not only eternal (ebedi), but it is also pre-eternal (ezeli). For many of you, this last statement means Rabb (the Lord/Creator).

So, existential philosophy can actually be explained by traveling at the speed of light. But if we consider that the universe was initially composed only of energy, wouldn't today's universe have been formed if an infinitely small mass, formed by the concentration of that energy at a point, traveled at the speed of light with that energy? In short, the entire meta-universe was actually that massive particle created by the concentration of that initial energy at a point. Anyone can ask whatever they wish on this matter within the framework of logic.

Now let's come to why and how that energy emerged. There is no scientific explanation at this point. You will either accept that that energy was created by Rabb, or you will deny your own existence. There is no third way. This is the short summary of existence. I am open to questions within the framework of logic.
