Quantum Computing Logic and Development Proposals

If you are interested in computer technologies, the impossibility of sustaining computer science solely through classical coding logic must have crossed your mind. Current studies are mostly focused on application speed and visualization technologies; however, if all sciences are truly interconnected, how can the isolation of computer science be explained? Why should positive sciences like physics and mathematics be separated from computer science by such distinct lines?

After all, has not computer science been derived through connection points linked via web development tools, which are themselves bound by electromagnetic waves on an information-processing basis? Would it not be more accurate to place this under the sub-field of electromagnetism within the domain of physics? Let these questions suffice for now, and let us move on to the answers.

If we consider cognitive systems to be interconnected via electromagnetic waves, instantaneous data transmission through quantum radiation becomes possible. Instead of utilizing today's web protocols, if we base our approach on the core principles of quantum physics—specifically the philosophical foundation that a "meta" (entity) can exist in different locations simultaneously—we could enable an electromagnetic wave to exist at two different ends of the world at once. Furthermore, by placing a quantum connection point inside a spacecraft like Voyager for space missions, we could ensure instantaneous data reception from outside the galaxy.

In fact, through this path, future studies could be conducted on the ability of "meta"—which is essentially a different form of electric current—to exist in multiple locations simultaneously. From this perspective, it could also offer an alternative solution for teleportation and initiate cloning research.

I believe this subject is of critical importance. For those who wish to develop technology, a collective effort can be unified to conduct research within this scope.
