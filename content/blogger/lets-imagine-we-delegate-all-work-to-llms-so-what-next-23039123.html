Friends, does it ever cross your mind why human beings were created bound by moral rules, and what kind of chaos the world would be in if moral rules never existed at all?

Furthermore, even if today’s automated humans truly believe that technological progress is something that must happen, can they actually fathom how far technology might advance? Are they aware that if the artificial decision-making devices they produce under the name of LLMs develop as much as they are meant to and then advance even further, self-programming robots will emerge? If this happens, who can guarantee that these robots will program themselves in a harmless format?

After all, if these robots—unbound by any moral rules—develop themselves far further and eventually decide to take over the entire world, who will be able to stop them? Are we going to entrust the fate and condition of humanity to robots devoid of any conscientious responsibility, and is everyone truly aware of what will happen if this comes to pass?

In short, what I mean to say is this: “Rules must immediately be imposed on robot development, and no one on Earth should be exempt from these rules. Otherwise, we will truly have prepared our own doomsday ourselves. All people must make peace, and humanity must not be forced to develop ever more robots for defense. If this does not happen, we will have prepared our own apocalypse ourselves. Please let this be heeded.”
