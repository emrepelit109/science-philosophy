Most of us have wondered, what exactly are dark energy and dark matter, which constitute about 97% of the universe, and what do they do? I wonder what the relationship is between dark matter and dark energy.

As I wrote in my previous blogs, the universe is actually a software. And as those of you who write code will know, every coding system has the ability to open a comment line. The comment lines of the universe software may also be the structures known as dark matter that allow the universe to stay together.

There was something else I stated in my previous articles; by taking advantage of features such as the quantization of energy, I characterized energy as the concept that matter itself is composed of. A conclusion that can be drawn from this is that dark energy is still the initial state of dark matter. And as the universe expands, dark energy gradually transforms into dark matter, helping to ensure the integrity of the universe. This may be a system automated by Allah according to the necessity analysis in the system established by Allah. So, in fact, we have no chance of solving all the mysteries of the universe because we do not have the chance to create dark matter.




Even if we observe the effects of dark energy as enabling the universe to expand by accelerating, we do not have the chance to produce this energy through natural means. In this case, the idea that we can know the secrets of the universe is nothing but a fallacy.

So what void does dark energy fill in the universe software? We can think of this as libraries that have not yet been activated. We can say that when each library is activated, the parameters within it are included in the universe software and find a place for themselves in the universe as entities that we can perceive. In this respect, we can interpret the universe as a system whole that is determined deterministically.
