                     The Art of Creation and What Can Be Done




Friends, I'm sure most of us have wondered: Could a stage be reached in the process of technological advancement where technology allows us to create something out of nothing—in other words, how far can the boundaries of technology reach?

The answer is quite obvious for those who have read my previous blog posts in depth and pondered a bit: If we manage to utilize the HIGGS field, we can endow electric charges with whatever mass we desire and obtain massive particles. But there is a small issue: while the HIGGS field is sometimes massive, it is also sometimes in the form of energy.

In this case, it can be said that just as sunlight causes tanning in skin pigments—producing visible effects—we could build upon such effects to resolve the energy-matter duality and find a way to utilize the HIGGS field even when it is in its energy form. The art of creation certainly wouldn't be limited to just this if that TRL (Technology Readiness Level) can be achieved; perhaps it could become so much more.

So, I think the art of creation can be summarized this way. I would also love to hear your thoughts.
