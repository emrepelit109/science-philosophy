We have all noticed that during the sleep process, our consciousness remains in the background, which is nothing like the resting process while awake. This is my explanation for it:

As I mentioned in my previous articles, the body in this world has only as much importance as an avatar of our soul. In other words, it is like a computer system guided by the soul, which is the "user." If we liken this computer system to our present-day computers, we can model the closure of our consciousness during sleep as the prioritized selection of the system's backend communication with the server. In this process, we can think as if the frontend of the computer is shut down and the backend part continues to work. The backend side communicates with the main server, and our dreams may be the feedback we receive from the user (our soul) during this communication.

Combining this with the idea that we remember some of our dreams and do not remember others when we wake up, we can conclude that sometimes these experiences are sent as a coded array using only cryptology techniques, without visualization.

That is to say, even when communication with the soul is not established through acts of worship, we may still be transferring information to the soul during sleep.

Such topics can be detailed further. Looking from this perspective, our opportunity to give meaning to life increases somewhat. These are my personal thoughts; in other words, it is nothing to be misunderstood.
