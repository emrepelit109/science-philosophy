The Theory of Everything


Most of us have heard of the question: "What Could Be the Theory of Everything?". In this context, the most positive answer might be: "How Many Dimensions Are Black Holes?". If this is solved, space-time studies will truly transition to a new phase.

Then, I would like to state this idea of mine, which is based on solid foundations: "Black holes are not bottomless pits; they just appear to be holes to us because their gravity increases. We cannot perceive color because light photons cannot escape. We accept their density as infinite only because we cannot calculate it, but they are actually very large, point-like massive particles."

Therefore, since they do not have a relationship with infinity, they do not contradict the statement "God is infinite" found in the Quran. Only God can be infinite.

Actually, when you think of my blogs as a single piece, you can find a detailed explanation of most concepts considered important today. The space-time plane is just a part that is connected to different physical dimensions via wormholes, which I mentioned in my previous writings. Parallel universes are real because the deformity in the geometric shapes of stars, mentioned in my previous blog, proves that the energy distribution during the Big Bang was not homogeneous, which in turn indicates that something existed before the Big Bang.

That is, our universe is not alone in the cosmos. We can prove this in many different ways, but I will write about them in my future blogs.
