                           Is Reality One-Dimensional and Indestructible?



Most of us—especially those who deeply examine the articles on my blog—have probably wondered: “Why was it said that ‘This universe is not real; in fact, the universe we are in is so fake that the only reality is our soul, which is a portion of God’s spirit,’ and what is its degree of truth?” Let us examine this question through the Bekenstein-Hawking interpretation and the principle that an object falling into a black hole’s event horizon cannot cease to exist from non-existence—meaning it transforms into entropy by increasing the black hole's entropy.

I do not know if I need to explain this, but a material quantity falling into a black hole first disintegrates into its smallest particles and then transforms into energy by merging with the particle that evolves into its antimatter. Just like the symmetry of the Big Bang. Later, that energy integrates with the energy of the black hole, and cumulative entropy increases. Simply put, it is like the reverse evolution of the Big Bang. In basic terms, this universe is heading toward the energy state of the Big Bang. That is why the total entropy of the universe is always on the rise. And precisely for this reason, religion is sent: to reduce that entropy when total entropy reaches its maximum level. This is another proof of God, though naturally, some will not understand, and anyone interested can ask questions about this in the comments.

Now let us come to the one-dimensional structure of what is real. Many of us accept the holographic principle thesis as true, yet most of us have not objected to the hypothesis advocated in this thesis—that there must be a 2-dimensional reality.

Now, if this 3-dimensional universe we are in is actually a simulation of the 2-dimensional reality at the boundaries of black holes, have you thought about what that 2-dimensional reality is a simulation of? Someone observing black holes from the outside can, of course, see a 2-dimensional gravitational field; but what does the structure of the point-like particle at the center of the black hole have to do with its field of influence? If black holes are the reverse symmetry of the Big Bang, wouldn't they try to reduce their total entropy? That is, the main purpose of black holes is not to swallow matter, but to empty the entropy inside them. However, their mass is so huge that they can never achieve this.

Perhaps those who have read my previous articles have gradually realized: I have tried to describe God with attributes like Al-Wahid (The One) and Al-Qadir (The Omnipotent). What does this mean? God is far from entropy and has an unchanging nature; that is, no power is sufficient to change God. Furthermore, Jannat al-Ala (the Highest Paradise) is also completely isolated from entropy—this is the conclusion that emerges from the Holy Qur'an.
