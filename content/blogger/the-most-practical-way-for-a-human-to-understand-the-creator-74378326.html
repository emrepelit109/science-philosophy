The Most Practical Way for a Human to                         Understand the Creator

Friends, I don’t know if anyone has thought about this, but verse 16 of Surah Qaf states: “And We have already created man and know what his soul whispers to him, and We are closer to him than his jugular vein.” I believe it is also possible to interpret this verse as follows: Allah (SWT) has determined His distance from us in proportion to our distance from the feeling of love, and has made the world more lovable for the people of the Quran. In other words, Allah is actually as far from us, His servants, as the feeling of love—no more, no less.

In fact, so that our feeling of love may be continuous and last until the end of our lives, Allah first introduced Himself in the Quran as the One who most absolutely deserves love; then, for those who cannot come to know Him through the Quran alone, He gave examples from His creation and advised us to study and research created beings in order to know Him.

Today, I sadly observe that the majority of people who say they are Muslim live merely to pass the days. Yet, the Prophet of Islam, Muhammad (PBUH), set a goal worth living for by saying in his hadith, “The best among you is the one who sets a good example for those around them,” and advised us to take lessons from the Quran.

In the Holy Quran, research topics are exemplified for Muslims through various plants, animals, celestial bodies, and other creations, encouraging Muslims to advance in scientific fields. For instance, Allah gave a fly as an example in the Quran, yet Muslims have reached a point where they learn even the structural characteristics of a fly from other civilizations. Many similar examples could be listed.

As for the actual reason I am writing this piece: Allah has granted Muslims the greatest blessing by enjoining love and pointing people first to Allah, and then to all of creation, as beings to love. Indeed, if we truly succeed in loving Allah, the knot in our jugular vein will untie, and we will be able to look at all beings through the eyes of love.

In short, love is a reward not only for the beloved, but also for the one who loves, and Allah has rewarded the ummah of the final prophet by sending this religion.
