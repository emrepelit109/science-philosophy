To our current knowledge, the universe is four-dimensional: consisting of length, width, height, and time. But have we ever considered how many dimensions time itself might have? Why hasn't it occurred to us to investigate quantities like time travel—which sounds like mysticism today—or the very structure of time? Furthermore, why is it that low-mass material particles like electrons can approach the universal speed limit—the speed of light—so closely, while massive objects like planets and stars accelerate and decelerate only within limited ranges? Even if people claim the sole reason is the effect of electromagnetic attraction and formulate it in various ways, could there be another physical quantity at play?

My view on this matter is that time actually consists of particles millions of times smaller than a lepton, and as matter gets smaller, it approaches the dimension of time, thereby gaining the potential for time travel. In other words, I believe leptons can move so close to the speed of light precisely because they are near the particle scale of time. Recall that God brings anything He wills into existence by simply saying, "BE." Since God is the Creator of all things and the Creator of time itself, He controls the very particles that constitute time. Through His attribute of Takwin (Creation), He organizes these particles, brings them forth from nothingness, and can perform anything He wills. This could be the physical explanation for it.

Similarly, I believe that photons, which constitute light rays, are able to travel at the speed of light due to their proximity in size to these "time particles."

Could there be a material particle smaller than a photon, or are speeds faster than light possible?

My answer is yes, because the quantum teleportation experiments at CERN involved phenomena occurring faster than the speed of light. Therefore, photons are material entities that cannot be considered as "nothingness" in terms of size. And there exist even smaller material entities: the particles of time.

If microscopes advanced enough are ever produced, the dimensional analysis of time will eventually be possible.
