                                 The Dual Nature of Time




Most of us might think of space as nothing more than an absolute zero point where energy resides, and as it is already known, the phenomenon we call energy is attracted or warped by the gravity of massive bodies. To provide an example, it is known that black holes pull objects behind the event horizon with slightly less energy than those at the event horizon. Here, it can be observed that the gravity of black holes accelerates or decelerates the energy of attraction. In short, this proves once again that energy, too, has material properties.

Returning to our subject, space is an absolute zero point containing no material quantities other than energy, and it is clear that every point within it possesses the same structure; therefore, we can say that space is a low-density energy fragment. Some might argue that it is illogical for energy to concentrate at certain points to form a tangible tunnel or gateway between different regions of space. However, this does not deny the reality of Wormholes. It merely supports a thesis that Wormholes are not massive tunnels or tangible gateways in a literal sense, but rather a mechanism where the flow of time performs a kind of U-turn.

For this to be observed in a real sense, a massive particle must be moved at a speed sufficiently close to the speed of light. Quantum teleportation experiments at CERN also show us that if a massive particle reaches speeds near the speed of light, it exits the four-dimensional plane of the universe and can travel independently of time. In fact, what is called a Wormhole is precisely such a thing.




As for the topic of time, I have mentioned in my previous writings that time possesses a nature beyond our imagination. Have you ever considered that Einstein-Rosen bridges are actually instances where time folds back upon itself, that traveling back in time might be possible, that teleportation could be quite simple through this mechanism, and that this mechanism might even be a shortcut to existence? As I said, all that needs to be done may be to move massive particles at speeds close to the speed of light and teleport them to the desired destination.
