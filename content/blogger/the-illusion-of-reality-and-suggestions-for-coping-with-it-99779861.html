                                   The Illusion of Reality and                             Suggestions for Coping with It

           


Many of us have noticed that when we limit reality strictly to what we observe, many things we believe to be true remain unanswered. For instance, the First Cause Paradox cannot explain exactly which reactants reacted to form the universe. Similarly, the question of "Why did life begin on Planet Earth, and why didn't a life form compatible with local conditions emerge on another planet?" still sparks curiosity, and scientists are still searching for life on other planets.

This means, in truth, there is an vast amount of information in the universe that is beyond our reach, which gives rise to a need for belief. Since we cannot access all the knowledge in the universe, instead of trying to answer these questions with speculative data, having a religious belief becomes essential. At this point, it is a matter of personal freedom to examine the holy book of each Abrahamic religion and investigate when it took its final form throughout history. By doing so, one can compare the level of knowledge contained within the book against the general level of knowledge available at the time of its final compilation. This process will ultimately reveal whether the book proposes ideas that are more advanced than the accumulated knowledge of that specific historical era.

If a person conducts this research objectively, they can clearly see that the only holy book whose claims remain unrefuted—even by today's accumulated knowledge—is the Holy Quran. Had the Bible (Gospel), the Torah, or the Psalms been preserved exactly as they were first revealed to their respective prophets, the information within them would also be unrefutable. However, Matthew, Luke, John, Mark, and others did the greatest disservice to their faiths; they stripped them of being divine religions and turned them into arbitrary, self-styled belief systems. But as I mentioned, the only holy book that still cannot be disproven is the Holy Quran, and according to a verse in the Quran, Allah has declared that it will be protected until the Day of Judgment.

In conclusion, it is evident that the Quran is still preserved exactly as it was originally sent down. In this regard, people should distance themselves from the corrupted religions outside of Islam and strive to read and learn the unknown secrets and unknowable knowledge of the universe from the Quran as much as they can. Humanity must move away from whims and desires, and instead seek to draw lessons from the Quran. In fact, all knowledge is present in the Quran. People just need to read it more carefully—not merely for the sake of reading, but by contemplating deeply over it.

For example, as a result of my extensive readings, my conclusion is this:

"Allah is the only Absolute Reality; everything else is merely an illusion."

Doesn't it catch your attention that everything (even time) is relative? Don't you agree that if we are talking about existence, there must be a constant, absolute reality?
