I am sure most people might not have understood anything from the title, but after the things I will explain throughout the article, everything will fall into place. As each of us can understand, the fact that we can change even the slightest thing shows that creation is not entirely deterministic, but rather that certain variables are connected to each other through calculations at the quantum level. In other words, parameters are linked to one another by the lengthy and sequential repetition of connectives in classical logic, such as and, or, if...then, either...or, and not.

To clarify what I mean: For instance, the occurrence of rain happens through the coming together of numerous variables. These variables are connected to each other via logical connectives, and this mechanism is fundamentally brought together by logic gates like XOR, NOR, NOT, etc., found in electronic circuits. Given that closed-circuit electronic devices or capacitor-based apparatuses are developed today, the Creator may have designed this universe as a self-sufficient device, setting this order in motion with an initiating energy, and may have crafted a circuit design and created the initial energy for each of the other 80,000 realms.

That is to say, the Creator will not alter the order unless we humans invoke His name and seek His help. What we humans call "free will" is actually the capability to change the order in line with one's purposes, and this capability exists in no other being except the Creator and us humans. All other entities are quantities operating on the logic of electronic circuits that I mentioned at the beginning. If we adapt Allah's statement in the holy book, the Quran, "Allah is closer to His servants than their jugular vein," to this concept, the mechanism of will (istenç) is a divine light (nur) granted to us humans from Allah. When we combine this with the verse where the human heart is held superior to the 7 universes, the view reinforces that the 7 universes are merely a pure electronic circuit, and the mechanism of will is a mechanism bestowed uniquely upon humans.

In the absence of the mechanism of will, a human transforms into a closed-circuit electronic device. What endows humans with additional value is this sense of will.
