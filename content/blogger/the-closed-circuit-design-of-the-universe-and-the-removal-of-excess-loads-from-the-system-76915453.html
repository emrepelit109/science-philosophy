We humans likely function as the current in this closed electronic system. In other words, who can say that two people who appear to be moving towards each other are actually approaching one another within a network of connectivity? True, current flows in a single direction in electronic circuits, but couldn't we consider that separate electronic circuits have been established for each individual, and that every person can move within their own circuit?

If you think about it a little, I am sure you could say that the quantity called "Destiny" (Kader) is the conductive wire within which each of us moves. In this model, we can comfortably say that what we call "Fate" (Kaza) is the capability of us—acting as electric currents—to choose one of the crossroads with equivalent resistance.

Now let's come to the entirety of the universe for which we made the closed-circuit modeling. Perhaps you have noticed, in this closed-circuit universe model, at points where the electric charge is excessive (where entropy is highly concentrated), logically there must be an open switch so that the excess entropy can discharge there. It is not difficult to guess the equivalent of this for our model: "Black holes."

Now that we have established the open switch modeling, let's come to the question of how Allah (SWT) transformed this model into the universe we live in. Actually, let's try to make a much smaller prototype of it in a virtual environment. First, let's make a Pac-Man style human modeling. What happens if this Pac-Man eats all the pellets that come its way? It meets a bad end very quickly. What happens if it eats no pellets at all? The game very quickly realizes that Pac-Man wants to bypass the game through deception and confronts it with a bad end in a place where there are no pellets. So how does Pac-Man find the right path? If the map of the game is given to it, it reaches the end without falling into any traps. Well, if we modeled real life with the game of Pac-Man, how do we find the map of the game in real life?
