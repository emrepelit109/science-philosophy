Quantum Entanglement and the                          Fabric of the Universe: The                                     Space-Time Illusion

Those who know will remember: quantum entanglement is the phenomenon where the relative states of two objects at different points in the universe can change within certain limits in relation to each other, or in other words, influence one another. In this case, I suppose the following thought has crossed everyone's mind: "So, is the distance between two objects merely an illusion, meaning was everything created in pairs and we just fail to realize it?"

Let us think a bit deeper on this matter: "What kind of explanations do belief systems offer for this situation, and could there be a connection between this phenomenon and belief systems?" For example, to give an instance from the Islamic faith, the Quran states, "We created everything in pairs." Muslims often interpret this as meaning that only living beings, including non-hermaphroditic ones, are created with their respective mates; however, in the Quran, this is expressed as every entity, and it is obvious that there must be a different interpretation of this situation.

In short, if we speak according to the Quran, it is illogical for Almighty Allah, the creator of the entire universe and the hereafter, to dedicate extra space in the holy book just to explain this obvious fact—especially in a holy scripture like the Holy Quran, where new verses are constantly being clarified day by day.

I mean to say, was our Lord, the Creator of the universe, unaware of quantum entanglement when He revealed this verse? On the contrary, I believe what this verse is telling us is that every entity is connected to another entity through a bond of entanglement. Furthermore, if we take this a step further, it can be thought that prophets might have been created in entanglement with an entity in Paradise, while other humans are entangled with one of the entities in the universe.

Starting from this point, since we cannot know which entity we are entangled with, I believe we must show absolute respect to every single being and never exhibit aggressiveness or anger.
