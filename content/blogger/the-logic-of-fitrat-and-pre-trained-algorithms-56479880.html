THE LOGIC OF FITRAT AND PRE-TRAINED ALGORITHMS

Most of us have probably wondered: "Even if we say that the Lord created us along with all other beings, what could be the way the Lord knows varying human decisions from the very first moment of creation, or what is the way He knows for sure the situations of the world that change with our decisions from the very first moment He created them?" Actually, the answer is explained in detail in our holy book, the Holy Quran.

In the Quran, in Surah Ar-Rum, Ayah 30, according to Ömer Çelik's translation: "Therefore, oh Messenger! Fix your entire being upon the true religion of Islam, in a pure, monotheistic faith, away from every kind of false belief! This religion is to submit to the only God, who is the ultimate goal of God's creating humanity, has no partner, is all-powerful, possesses infinite knowledge and wisdom. There can be no change in the laws that God has created and made dominant over existence, and in the foundational beliefs He has sent down. This is the upright and perfect religion. However, most people do not know this." He says.

That is, Allah (C.C.) actually says openly that He created all humans upon Islam and explains the basic criteria of Islam. And He states that there will be no change in these foundational beliefs, so that any Muslim should not think: "This religion was sent so many years ago, God's commands have changed."

Actually, this verse, which is the summary of the Islamic religion, also gives very important information about the creation of humans. As I mentioned in my previous writings, reason is the central processing unit of the human hardware, and the heart is the quantum processor unit to which reason is integrated. And at this point, if we think of reason as being programmed in the most ideal way, the origin of this program is the concept we call 'fitrat,' and every code snippet of fitrat is a whole set of software that has been 'pre-trained.'

Because the primary factor enabling us humans to make our decisions is our heart, the only part that our soul, to which our heart is connected, can influence is a very small decision-making option that our reason (CPU) cannot reach. In other words, what we call 'cüz’i irade' (partial free will) is exactly this.

Now, think about it if you will, if we humans, after maximizing the information in our minds, also bypass our mind's decision-making processes without disconnecting from our mind, could we manifest some of our Rabb's essential attributes in our selves, think about that if you will, okay?
