Matter and Consciousness Dilemma: Is Telepathy Possible?

To make sense of my thoughts on this subject, I believe it is necessary to grasp my thesis, which I have tried to place in a large part of my previous writings: Actually, Everything is Connected and the Universe is Actually a Network of Interconnections Woven with a Web.

We can say that all particular entities are nothing but informational bits in the interconnection network of the universe, and these bits are connected to each other over a network. While some bits are connected in a prioritized structure, connections that we cannot perceive have been established between others.

For example, the event of an apple falling from a tree to the ground is connected in a prioritized structure to the apple detaching from the branch, while the event of the Earth approaching and moving away from the Moon creating tides in the ocean is connected by an interconnection that humans only realized in the late 1700s. In other words, even if humans do not notice it, as in this example, everything in the universe continues its existence by being connected to each other.

When we think in this way, since our thoughts are also made up of bits, if we make even the smallest change in our thought structure, it is inevitable that the informational bit (entity) associated with our thoughts will also change. So, in fact, if we find which informational bit is associated with our thought structure, we might have the chance to make changes such as changing the position of that entity through thinking, or causing situational changes related to it. If we had the capacity to scan the entire universe, with a diameter of 13.7 billion light-years, we could find which matter is associated with our thought structure and have the chance to change it by thinking.
