Thoughts on Teleportation

Sometimes, we find ourselves wishing for the ability to become invisible. Let’s state from the beginning that this is far more challenging for living organisms. However, while we cannot yet know if future technologies will grant this to the rest of the meta-universe, it might be possible to quantize a solid mass (meaning, breaking it down to the quantum scale) and then reassemble those quantized particles anywhere else in the world to reconstruct that original object.

This would instantaneously reduce the logistics and shipping costs of high-value goods to zero. It is conceivable that these quantized particles could travel from one point to another via electromagnetic waves, much like the signals transmitted by a telegraph device.

In my previous blog posts, I mentioned that an object traveling at the speed of light could potentially change dimensions and reach eternity. Such a feat requires an incredibly advanced level of technology. Instead, focusing research on the aforementioned quantization method would be much more functional. Although achieving this today remains within the realm of imagination, even working toward this goal will contribute greatly to humanity.
