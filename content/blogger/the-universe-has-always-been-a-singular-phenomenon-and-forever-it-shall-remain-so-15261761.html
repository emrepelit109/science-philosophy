The Universe Has Always Been a Singular Phenomenon, and Forever It Shall Remain So

Many of us have wondered: "What was there before the Big Bang, or what was the phenomenon affected by the event in the Big Bang?" As I reflect on this question, inductive ideas come to mind.

For instance, if you have researched Islam even a little, you would know that Allah is described as "Vâhid," meaning One and Only. I thought of combining this concept with the possible cyclical existence-extinction hypothesis of the universe. That is, I am referring to a cyclical hypothesis where, since an unknowable time, one universal formation perishes while giving rise to the beginning of a new universal formation.

Then it occurred to me: in the Qur'an and authentic (sahih) hadiths, no precise number (such as 80,000) is specified regarding the vast number of worlds Allah created; such numbers mostly appear in weak narrations or commentaries. After reflecting on this, an idea emerged: does no development occur as universes perish and others are formed?

Later, the verses in the Qur'an regarding the superior creation of humanity came to my mind (such as Al-Isra 17/70 and At-Tin 95/4). Then I thought about the different natural traits humans are created with and asked, "Could we possess our current traits of creation in this universe because of what we did in the previous universe?"

In other words, if all human beings were initially created under equal conditions in the first of the successive universes, and then each person determined their traits of creation in the next universe through their behavior... then, even if we do not realize it, all people have actually come here by being weeded out from universes where they had equal opportunities. Therefore, people have no right to complain about any of their conditions.

Furthermore, if we could understand the Qur'an better, perhaps we could have reached many other pieces of knowledge.
