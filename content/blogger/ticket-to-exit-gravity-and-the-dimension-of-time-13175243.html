Ticket to Exit Gravity and the                                    Dimension of Time

As I have stated in my previous writings, the axis of time either envelops the universe like an ocean, or it is spread everywhere in space, independent of locality, with a discrete structure composed of minuscule particles called chronons. When considering my earlier articles on this subject, it is indeed possible to think of time as an ocean and our universe as a coral reef within that ocean. Furthermore, taking my previous writings into account, the only way to escape the classical 3-dimensional structure of the universe is to create a vortex in this cosmic ocean and wait for it to fling us outward. To achieve this, as I noted before, one must move fast enough in the ocean to generate a centrifugal force, thereby creating a deep-sea wave akin to a tsunami.

As you know, centrifugal force depends on two variables: mass and velocity. Naturally, in this equation, mass and velocity are inversely proportional to each other; since they are multiplied, as one increases, the other decreases. And as you well know, black holes are incredibly massive particles. When we consider the moving variants of these black holes, they could provide a way for us to poke our heads out by creating a vortex in the spacetime axis.

Now, the phenomenon of quantum teleportation at CERN shows us that a lepton requires an immense velocity to teleport relative to its mass. However, for a black hole to exist both in this universe and in outer dimensions, it does not even need to move—or moving at a very minimal velocity is sufficient.

Is this not exactly like the concept of Doomsday (Kiyamat) described to us in the holy book, the Quran? In other words, by stepping outside the concept of time at the moment of a black hole's gravitational pull, wouldn't we actually reach our eternal resting place? It is said that events will occur which people cannot explain with current laws of physics. Please consider the phenomena that could be experienced under the influence of a black hole.

Do Einstein-Rosen bridges not actually explain to us the states in which black holes move at any given velocity? Please, just think about it.
